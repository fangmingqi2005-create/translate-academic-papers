# Two-output contract

## Chinese full-text edition

Use the translated Chinese title. Include Chinese headings and every translated body block in source order. Use translated figures and tables. Keep equations and citation markers in place. Retain the English reference list.

## Paragraph-aligned bilingual edition

For each source block, use this order:

1. Exactly one English heading, caption, note, or body paragraph.
2. Exactly its complete Chinese translation immediately below.

Do not interleave sentence by sentence. Do not combine several English paragraphs into one Chinese block. Do not split one Chinese translation across unrelated English blocks. Do not omit short transition paragraphs, captions, notes, or Methods paragraphs. A source paragraph and its translation must share the same source ID and pair index.

Before delivery, run a pairing audit: the bilingual document must have `N` English source blocks and exactly `N` adjacent Chinese partners (excluding headings, references, and figure objects). For every pair, compare the source ID and verify that the Chinese block translates the same paragraph boundaries, citations, numbers, and sentence content. If extraction merges two source paragraphs, split them before translation; never compensate with a longer English block or several short Chinese blocks.

Use visible but restrained styling:

- English source paragraph: a subtle gray-blue label or border, original paragraph form, no first-line indentation.
- Chinese translation: normal body style with first-line indentation.
- Keep each English-Chinese pair together where practical, but allow natural page breaks for long pairs.
- Add stable hidden bookmarks or source IDs for pair auditing when useful.

In the Chinese-only edition, show the image-edited Chinese figure with Chinese internal labels and the Chinese caption.

In the bilingual edition, show both figures at each figure location in this order: the untouched English source figure with its English caption, then the image-edited Chinese figure with its Chinese caption. The Chinese figure must be produced by masking/replacing original English labels at the same locations, using the validated `image2` workflow when raster editing is needed.

Tables in the bilingual edition may use English cell text followed by Chinese in the same cell when space permits; otherwise place the English table first and the Chinese table directly after it. Preserve all values and notes.

Both editions must use the same reference numbering and English reference list.
