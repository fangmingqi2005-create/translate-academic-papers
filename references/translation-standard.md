# Translation standard

## Fidelity

Preserve the author's claims, not merely the topic. Match modality and strength: distinguish *may*, *suggest*, *is consistent with*, *demonstrates*, and *causes*. Keep negation, comparison groups, boundary conditions, and temporal order explicit. Never upgrade correlation to causation or a nonsignificant result to evidence of no effect.

Maintain a one-to-one source-block map. The Chinese-only edition may omit the visible English paragraph, but it must still be generated from the same paired source record as the bilingual edition.

## Chinese prose

Use fluent, formal Simplified Chinese suited to scholarly reading. Prefer established disciplinary terms. Avoid literal English word order, unnecessary passive constructions, excessive nominalization, and repetitive connectives. Preserve prose as prose; do not turn paragraphs into outline bullets unless the source is a list.

Use Chinese punctuation in Chinese prose while preserving mathematical notation, software syntax, identifiers, URLs, and reference formatting.

## Terminology and names

- Define an abbreviation once at first substantive use.
- Keep personal names in published Latin spelling unless a conventional Chinese form is useful.
- Keep study labels, model identifiers, condition codes, scale names, software versions, and dataset names searchable.
- Use the same Chinese term for the same construct throughout both deliverables and all figures.

## Citations and references

Keep every citation attached to the same claim in the English source and Chinese translation. Preserve numbers and ranges exactly. Do not fabricate missing DOI values or silently correct bibliographic data.

Retain the reference list in English. Give every numbered entry an ASCII bookmark and every numeric in-text citation a WPS-compatible internal hyperlink field. Make verified DOI strings clickable through `https://doi.org/<doi>`.

## Equations and end matter

Preserve operator precedence, indices, Greek letters, vectors, matrices, and numbering. Translate surrounding definitions, not mathematical expressions. Translate declarations and availability statements while preserving URLs, accession numbers, grant numbers, registry identifiers, author initials, and license names accurately.
